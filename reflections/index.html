How it works

Actual balance growth, not an estimated volume APR.

The dashboard measures reflection growth using an untouched 100 ALT benchmark wallet. For connected wallets, buys, sells and transfers are treated as checkpoints so those balance changes are not counted as passive reflection rewards. The APR shown is historical and annualised from the available tracking period — it is not a guaranteed future return.

Altitude Token · Base · Reflection analytics are informational and based on on-chain data.
